Welcome to ExpenseFlow! 👋

Your Personal, Offline-First Daily Ledger

  • 1. Welcome & About ExpenseFlow:
    ExpenseFlow is a lightweight personal finance tracker designed to help you manage your monthly allowance, track daily spending across Cash, UPI Lite, and UPI Bank, and automatically carry forward unspent balances every month.
  • 2. How Your Data Is Stored (Local & Google Drive):
    Everything you enter is saved immediately inside your own browser's LocalStorage so the app works completely offline. If you opt in to Google Drive Backup, it creates and syncs only one compact expense.json file inside your personal Drive—we cannot see, access, or read any other files in your Drive.
  • 3. 100% Privacy & Zero-Server Security:
    ExpenseFlow runs on a zero-server architecture. No developer server, database, or third-party tracker ever collects, reads, or sells your financial data—your numbers stay strictly between your device and your own Google account.
    Note: To keep your local data safe, avoid clearing your browser's site data/cache unless you have backed up to Google Drive or exported a CSV.
  • 4. Coming in Future Updates:
    Support for bank statement linking may be added in future versions to make expense tracking even faster.
